The challenge

Keep a critical service running

When a crisis hits — a supply-chain shock, a natural disaster, a major outage — the systems people depend on for food, medicine, and information can fail at the worst possible moment. Pick someone whose job is to keep that service running, then build an AI application that helps them do it.

Pick a persona

Invent a persona — a role, or a fictional person

Sparks, not a menu — name your own persona and your own angle.

  • Someone who routes food or medicine
    • Getting supplies to neighborhoods that get cut off first
  • Someone who keeps the public informed
    • Accurate information when the usual channels go down
  • Someone accountable for a critical service
    • A service that simply cannot be allowed to fail
Then build

The one thing that persona most needs

  • An automated routing / logistics agent
    • Keeps deliveries reaching vulnerable neighborhoods when supply lines break
  • A fail-safe communication app
    • Keeps people accurately informed during an outage or emergency
  • A continuity or triage system
    • Detects a disruption and acts automatically to reduce harm

Your idea doesn't have to be one of these. Find the one thing that makes your app genuinely useful, and grow it from there.
